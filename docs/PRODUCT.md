# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three roles: `client` (public signup, browses experts, books consultations), `member` (vetted
expert, application-gated, manages profile/availability/content), `admin` (internal, manages
applications, verification, and platform operations). Clients and members use the platform in
professional contexts — evaluating expertise, booking time, managing a paid membership — where
trust and clarity matter more than novelty.

## Product Purpose

Expertly is a membership platform connecting clients with vetted expert members: consultations,
peer-to-peer connect, articles, events, perks/templates/learnings, and tiered membership &
billing. Success looks like a platform that reads as credible and premium enough that experts want
to be associated with it and clients trust it enough to pay for access and book time with strangers.

## Brand Personality

Premium, clean, confident, unfussy. Closer to a professional network / paid-membership product
(Linear, Vercel, Stripe) than a consumer marketplace — restrained, generous whitespace, no
gimmicks.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS template look (stock hero-metric sections, identical icon+heading card grids,
gradient-text accents, glassmorphism). If it looks like it was assembled from a component
library's default examples, it's not done.

## Design Principles

- Premium and clean over playful or loud — the design should read as trustworthy to both sides of
  a paid marketplace.
- Generous whitespace over dense information packing.
- Mobile-first responsive — every surface must hold up at 375px and 1440px, not just desktop.
- Consistency across the three roles' surfaces (client, member, admin) — same design language,
  different information density.
- No AI-slop tells: no side-stripe borders, no gradient text, no decorative glassmorphism, no
  hero-metric template, no identical card grids, no reflexive modals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: text contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, semantic HTML and proper
heading hierarchy, labeled form inputs with real error messaging, alt text on meaningful images.
